# RingLight — Selfie Fill Light Camera

RingLight turns your phone's screen into a soft, colored ring light. Light your face from the front, take bright selfies in the dark, shoot hands-free with the timer and burst, pick the keepers before anything hits your gallery, and use the built-in makeup mirror or full-screen lamp anytime.

## Highlights

- **Soft screen fill light** — curated color presets plus custom colors; much softer than the front flash
- **Hands-free timer & burst** — 3/5/10-second countdown, up to 10 shots per take, adjustable intervals; the light stays on the whole time
- **Pick before you save** — photos land in an in-app tray first; only the keepers go to your gallery
- **Mirror mode** — a true-reflecting makeup mirror with its own fill light; tap to freeze and inspect
- **Full-screen lamp** — steady, breathing, strobing, or rhythmic light, plus half-screen side light
- **Real camera controls** — brightness/exposure gestures, mirrored preview, 3:4 / 9:16 / 1:1 framing

## FAQ

**Q: Does the app upload my photos?**
No. Photos stay on your device. They are only written to your photo library when you choose to save them.

**Q: Do I need an account?**
No. No sign-up, no login.

**Q: Are there ads or watermarks?**
No ads and no watermarks.

**Q: Why is my screen brightness changed?**
The app raises screen brightness to make the fill light effective, and restores your original brightness automatically when you leave.

**Q: My photos are in the app but not in the gallery — where are they?**
Captured photos wait in the in-app tray. Open the tray from the gallery button, pick the keepers, and save — only then do they appear in your photo library.
