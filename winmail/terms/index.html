Legacy mail formats such as TNEF (winmail.dat), MSG, PST, OST and OLM are complex, closed, and imperfectly documented. Winmail Reader decodes them on a best-effort basis: we do not guarantee that every file can be opened or that decoding is error-free. Damaged, password-protected, encrypted, or otherwise protected files may fail to decode. When decoding fails, the app's health check gives an explainable diagnosis (what the file really is, and what to do next) instead of a silent failure. Keep your own backups of important originals.
