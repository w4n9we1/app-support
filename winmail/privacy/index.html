Privacy Policy — Winmail Reader

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Winmail Reader ("we", "us", or "our") is built to open email files without putting your data at risk. This Privacy Policy explains what the app handles, what it never collects, and your choices. The short version: your files are decoded on your device and never uploaded.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS app Winmail Reader - DAT, MSG, EML on iPhone and iPad, published by W4N9WE1 / Lababacy.

2. Data We Process

2.1 Your files — processed on your device only

  • Winmail Reader decodes winmail.dat (TNEF), MSG, OFT, EML, MHT/MHTML, XPS, OXPS, MBOX, PST, OST and OLM files entirely on your device. There is no Winmail Reader server, and no upload step.
  • Your original files are opened read-only — the app never modifies them. ("Your originals are never modified.")
  • Decoded copies (extracted text, attachments, exports) are written only into the app's own sandbox on your device, where you can view, share, or delete them.

2.2 Permissions

  • The app requests no standing permissions — no camera, microphone, location, photo library, contacts, or calendar access is held while you use it.
  • Calendar and contacts, one exception by design: if you tap Add to Calendar or Save to Contacts on a decoded invitation or attachment, the specific data you selected is handed to the system editor screen for you to review and confirm. The app itself does not read your calendar or your contacts, before or after.

2.3 Settings stored on your device

Your preferences, the recent-files list, and browsing history are stored locally on your device. You can delete the recent list and history from inside the app at any time (see section 8).

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, sign-up, or email address required
  • No file uploads — there is no server that could receive them
  • No file contents in analytics: no file names, no message subjects, no search terms
  • No access to your contacts or calendar databases
  • No advertising or tracking — the app contains no ad SDKs and never asks permission to track you
  • No behavioral profiles, and we do not sell personal data

4. No Ads, No Tracking

Winmail Reader contains no advertising and no ad SDKs. We do not serve ads, do not show any ad-related consent forms or prompts (no consent management platform, no App Tracking Transparency request), and do not include any ad-privacy entry in the app. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Your files are never used for advertising — there are no ad requests at all.

5. Purchases

Winmail Reader Pro is available as a one-time lifetime purchase or an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription, processed by Apple as App Store In-App Purchases. When you purchase, restore, or check entitlement, RevenueCat acts as our service provider and may process purchase records, transaction information, entitlement status, and a pseudonymous app user ID. RevenueCat does not receive your files and has no way to see them. We never receive or store your payment card details.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's privacy information for how these providers handle purchase-related data.

6. Crash Reports and Usage Analytics

  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports, which may include device model, operating-system and app versions, and stack traces, to diagnose and fix crashes.
  • Firebase Analytics records a limited set of usage events (for example, that a tool was opened, with counters and an app-instance identifier). Event parameters are only closed enumerations and counts — they do not include file names, message subjects, search terms, file contents, or personal information.
  • These analytics are not used for advertising or to build behavioral profiles.

Google processes this information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the processors described above — Google (Firebase), Apple (App Store and purchases), and RevenueCat (purchase entitlement) — or where the law requires it.

8. Local Data and Retention

  • Temporary decoded copies are stored only inside the app's private sandbox and are kept in a rolling window of the most recently used copies — they are never shared.
  • Deleting an entry from the recent list does not delete its decoded copy.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it — files, decoded copies, settings, and history.

9. Children

Winmail Reader is not directed at children, and we have no knowledge of collecting personal information from children.

10. Your Choices

  • Delete a recent-files entry by swiping it in the recent list on the home screen.
  • The app contains no ads and no tracking — there are no ad-consent choices to manage.
  • Restore a purchase with Restore Purchases in the app; manage or refund purchases through Apple.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Questions or requests: email us (below).

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: email the developer.
