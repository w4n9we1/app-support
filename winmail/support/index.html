Winmail Reader Support

Need help opening winmail.dat, MSG, EML or an Outlook archive? Start here.

Quick start — open your first file

  1. From Mail: open the message with the winmail.dat attachment, then tap Share (or tap the attachment) and choose Winmail Reader.
  2. From Files: locate the file, tap Share — or long-press it and choose Share — then pick Winmail Reader.
  3. From any app's share sheet: tap Share and send the file to Winmail Reader. Decoding happens instantly on your device — nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

What is a winmail.dat file?

When Outlook sends a message in TNEF format, it packs the body, attachments and calendar invites into a single winmail.dat file. Other mail clients see only that one unopenable file. Winmail Reader unwraps the package and gives you back the message text, the attachments and the calendar invitation.

How do I open winmail.dat on iPhone or iPad?

In Mail or Files, tap Share (or Open In) and choose Winmail Reader, or send the file over from another app's share sheet. Decoding happens instantly on your device.

Is Winmail Reader free?

Yes. The free download reads every supported format in full, shows attachment lists and previews archives. Exporting and converting (PDF, EML, HTML, TXT), extracting attachments, search inside mail and full archive browsing are part of Pro — unlocked with a one-time lifetime purchase, pay once and keep it forever. An optional monthly subscription is also available, priced as shown in the app. No quotas.

Do you upload my files?

No. Decoding is 100% on-device: no account, no uploads. Your original file is opened read-only, and decoded copies stay inside the app's sandbox where you can delete them at any time. See the Privacy Policy.

I already bought Pro — how do I restore it?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you used for the purchase.

A file still won't open — now what?

Run the built-in health check: it explains what the file really is, how confident it is (magic bytes included), and what to do next. Still stuck? Email us (below).

Restoring a purchase

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. Reinstall the app for free, then choose Restore Purchases in the app's settings. If it does not unlock, make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

If you chose the monthly subscription, it renews automatically until cancelled — you can manage or cancel it at any time in your App Store account settings. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the end of the current period stops future renewals; Pro stays available through the end of the period you already paid for.
