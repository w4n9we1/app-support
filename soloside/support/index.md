## Support FAQ

**Q: Do I need an account?**
A: No. There is no account, no email, no sign-up. SoloSide opens straight into your record, and nothing is uploaded to us. If you enable sync, your record goes to your own private iCloud, not to a SoloSide server.

**Q: Does the other parent need to install SoloSide?**
A: No. SoloSide is one-sided by design. You record your own parenting time; the other parent is never contacted, never notified, and never given access.

**Q: How do I record a handoff?**
A: Tap "Log a handoff", pick one of six statuses (on time, late, no show, cancelled, rescheduled, denied), confirm the time, and optionally add a place, photos and a note. It takes about thirty seconds and works offline.

**Q: How do I fix a mistake in an entry?**
A: Open the entry and edit it. The original value and the correction are both kept, with timestamps, and the entry's fingerprint changes. Corrections never overwrite history — that is deliberate, because a record that can be silently rewritten is worth less to you.

**Q: The PDF export failed. What now?**
A: Exports are generated offline, so a failure is usually local: storage space running out, or the app being killed mid-export. Free space first and retry — your draft and entries are kept. If it fails twice, send us the report from Settings → Feedback and tell us the record count and iOS version.

**Q: Which files can I attach?**
A: Photos (including HEIC), scanned receipts and PDFs. The free tier allows three attachments per entry; Pro removes the limit. Corrupted or unreadable files are rejected with an explanation rather than being stored silently.

**Q: Can I import my existing schedule?**
A: Yes. Export your calendar from Google Calendar as an .ics file and import it in the Calendar tab. The file is parsed on the device; nothing is uploaded. Entries that cannot be read are listed individually instead of failing the whole import.

**Q: How is the fingerprint / verification useful?**
A: Each entry's SHA-256 fingerprint is chained to the entry before it, so changing any character breaks the chain and SoloSide shows it. Pro adds an attorney export package with a hash list and an export ID that anyone can verify in a browser without installing the app.

**Q: How do I restore my purchase?**
A: Open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. If you bought Pro and see the paywall again after reinstalling, restore first before buying anything.

**Q: What is the difference between the monthly plan and the lifetime purchase?**
A: The lifetime purchase ($19.99, launch price $14.99) is the intended option: pay once, keep it. The monthly plan ($4.99/month) exists as a reference price, is billed monthly by Apple, and can be cancelled anytime in your Apple ID settings. Nothing is pre-selected.

**Q: How do I delete my data?**
A: Settings → Delete all data removes your records from the app after a confirmation, and deleting the app removes its local storage. Copies you exported or shared are outside the app — in Files, iCloud Drive, or wherever you sent them — and are controlled by you and those services.

**Q: Is SoloSide legal advice?**
A: No. SoloSide is a record-keeping tool. Whether a record is admitted in a case is decided by a judge under the rules of your jurisdiction. If something important is at stake, talk to a licensed attorney in your state or country.
