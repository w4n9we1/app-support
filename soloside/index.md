**Last Updated:** November 16, 2026

## What you log

A handoff takes about thirty seconds, one hand, standing at a school gate:

- **Six statuses** — on time, late, no show, cancelled, rescheduled, denied.
- **Time captured automatically**, with an optional place and distance.
- **Notes, photos and receipts** attached to the same entry.
- **Expenses** in exact cents with currency and category.
- **Custody factors** — about sixty structured tags family courts actually ask about.

Corrections never overwrite: the original entry stays, and the correction sits beside it with its own timestamp. Every entry carries a SHA-256 fingerprint chained to the entry before it, so a single changed character breaks the chain.

## Parenting time, counted

Custody schedules are rarely the problem; knowing what actually happened is. SoloSide shows scheduled versus actual by day, month and overnight count — so if the plan said 17 nights and you actually had 18, you see it before a mediator asks.

Schedule templates included: 50/50, 2-2-3, 2-2-5-5, week on week off, and 3-3-4-5, plus a custom builder, holidays that alternate by year, and single-day overrides when a swap happens. Existing schedules can be imported from a Google Calendar export (.ics) read entirely on the device.

## Export a custody PDF

One tap builds a court-ready PDF: cover, summary, integrity page, timeline, photos, expenses, custody factors, verification page and appendix. Each entry lists its timestamp and fingerprint. Exports are generated offline — no internet, no server, no submission queue.

Pro adds an attorney export package: a read-only bundle with an export ID and a hash list that anyone can verify in a browser, with no app and no account. You can revoke an export ID at any time; copies already delivered cannot be recalled, and the app says so plainly.

The PDF also comes in xlsx and calendar (.ics) form, and every record can be re-exported after an amendment.

## Works with nothing but your phone

There is no account, no sign-up, no invitation and no co-parent cooperation. SoloSide is built for one side of the arrangement — the parent who has to prove what happened. Nothing is uploaded: your record lives in the app's own storage, with optional sync to your own private iCloud, optional Face ID lock, and thirty days of automatic local snapshots if something goes wrong.

Chat screen recordings can be turned into readable text on the device — reviewed by you before anything is saved, and never uploaded.

## Who it is for

Parents in a custody dispute without an attorney, parents after separation who need an honest count of overnights, and anyone who has been asked "where is your record?" and had nothing but screenshots.

SoloSide is a record-keeping tool, not a law firm. Whether a record is admitted is decided by a judge under the rules where you live. What the app can do is make it provable that you wrote something when you wrote it, and that nobody quietly changed it afterwards.
