FAQ

Does it work without a Windows PC?

Yes. Parsing and rendering happen entirely on your device.

Can I convert a Publisher document to PDF on my phone?

Yes — open it and export as PDF. The text stays selectable and searchable.

What if a file will not open?

You get a clear explanation — password-protected, damaged or unsupported version — plus a diagnostic report. Never a blank screen, never a crash.

Is Publisher File Viewer free?

Viewing is free. Export and printing unlock with a single one-time purchase — no subscription.
