Privacy Policy — Publisher File Viewer

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Publisher File Viewer ("we", "us", or "our") is built to open Microsoft Publisher files without putting your data at risk. This Privacy Policy explains what the app handles, what it never collects, and your choices. The short version: your files are parsed on your device and never uploaded.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS app Publisher File Viewer on iPhone and iPad, published by W4N9WE1 / Lababacy.

2. Data We Process

2.1 Your files — processed on your device only

  • Publisher File Viewer parses .pub files (Microsoft Publisher, Publisher 97–2003 document containers) entirely on your device. There is no Publisher File Viewer server, and no upload step.
  • Your original files are opened read-only — the app never modifies them.
  • When you open a file, the app makes a copy inside its own sandbox on your device (so your original stays untouched); exports you create are written to the app's export folder, where you can view, share, or delete them. Deleting a document inside the app removes its copy.

2.2 Permissions

  • The app requests no standing permissions — no camera, microphone, location, photo library, contacts, or calendar access is held while you use it.
  • Documents are read only from files you pick in the system file picker or share into the app.

2.3 Settings stored on your device

Your preferences (appearance, reading theme, App Lock state), the recent-files list, favorites, reading positions and the conversion history are stored locally on your device. You can delete documents — and their copies — from inside the app at any time.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile.
  • No file contents, ever. Your documents, their text, and their images are never transmitted anywhere.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no third-party ad SDKs.
  • No behavioral profiling or cross-app tracking.

4. Purchases

The optional one-time unlock (and the optional monthly subscription) is processed entirely by Apple through App Store in-app purchase. Apple handles your payment details. Purchase receipts are validated through RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider, which stores an anonymous App Store receipt identifier to restore your purchase — never your files and never your identity beyond that identifier. Restore Purchases inside the app re-checks that identifier at any time.

5. Crash Reports and Usage Analytics

  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, a technical crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version) is sent via Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the cause. Crash reports do not include your file contents.
  • Anonymous conversion events. The app records two anonymous events — "an export finished" and "a purchase completed" — with basic counters only (for example, the export format). These events contain no file contents and no identifying information, and they are not linked to you.
  • We do not measure page views, sessions, or engagement, and we do not build profiles.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share data. The only processors are Apple (purchases, crash-report infrastructure via Firebase) and RevenueCat (anonymous receipt validation), each limited to the functions above.

7. Local Data and Retention

Everything the app stores lives in its sandbox on your device: document copies, exports, preferences, and the recent list. Deleting the app removes all of it. There is nothing stored on our side that could be handed over, because nothing identifiable reaches us.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose document tool, is not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone — including children.

9. Your Choices

  • Delete documents and exports from inside the app at any time.
  • Remove the app to erase every trace of local data.
  • Manage or cancel the monthly subscription in your App Store account settings; use Restore Purchases to re-apply a purchase on any device with the same Apple ID.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: email the developer from inside the app (Settings → Contact Support).
