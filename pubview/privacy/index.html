Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Privacy matters. This page explains, in plain language, what the app and this website do — and what they deliberately do not do. This site and the app are operated by Lababacy (lababacy.com). If anything here is unclear, write to w4n9we1@gmail.com.

The app — your files

Publisher File Viewer processes .pub files entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Your files are never uploaded to a server of ours — there is no server to upload them to.

The app is read-only by design: your original files are never modified. The app works without an account — no registration, no sign-in.

The app — diagnostics

Crash reports are collected through Firebase Crashlytics. A crash report contains technical information only: the stack trace, device model, operating system version and app version. Crash reports never include file content — we see the technical trace, not your document.

The app also records anonymous conversion-measurement events, such as the fact that an export completed. These events contain no file names and no file content.

The app — purchases

The one-time unlock and the optional monthly subscription are processed by Apple through App Store in-app purchase. Apple handles your payment details. Purchase receipts are validated through RevenueCat, our purchase-infrastructure provider, which stores an anonymous App Store receipt identifier so your purchase can be restored — never your files, and no identity beyond that identifier. Restore Purchases inside the app re-checks that identifier at any time.

Support email

When you write to w4n9we1@gmail.com for help, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to reply to your support request.

Website

This website is a set of static pages — plain HTML, CSS and images. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics scripts.
