6. Rendering Disclaimer

.pub is a legacy binary format and Microsoft has not published its specification. The app renders Publisher documents with high fidelity, but complex documents may contain elements it cannot reproduce exactly. When something cannot be rendered, the app shows a placeholder and a diagnostic note — it never silently drops content or invents a substitute. Export outputs (PDF/Word/RTF/TXT) are best-effort conversions intended for sharing and further editing, not archival substitutes for the original file.
