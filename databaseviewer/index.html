Frequently asked questions.

What files can it open?

Microsoft Access databases in MDB and ACCDB formats (Jet 3, Jet 4 and Access 2007+), SQLite databases, Excel XLSX and OpenDocument ODS spreadsheets, DBF (dBase III) files, CSV and TSV text data, and SQL dumps in the SQLite dialect — all in one app.

Is Database Viewer free?

Yes. Free lets you open files in every supported format, read the health report, view the first screen of your data, and use the full analysis modules. Pro — a single one-time purchase — unlocks the rest: full browsing, editing, SQL queries, export, file diff and cross-table search. An optional monthly subscription is also available, priced as shown in the app.

Do you upload my files?

No. Everything runs on your device: no account, no uploads, no ads, no tracking. Your original file is opened read-only, and edits happen on a private copy inside the app.

How large are the files it can handle?

The engine is native C, built for big data: tables with millions of rows and files of 1.5 GB and more stay smooth on your phone.

Can I edit my database?

Yes — editing is part of Pro. Edits are always made on a private copy inside the app; your original file is never modified.

What can I export?

With Pro you can export to CSV or XLSX, convert between formats including SQLite, or save the data back in its original format.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. Database Viewer is available on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 17+) and on Google Play for Android (8.0+) — the same product on both platforms.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Your purchase is tied to the Google Account you bought it with. Install the app on the new device, sign in with the same account, and Pro is recognized automatically — you are never charged twice. If anything looks off, email w4n9we1@gmail.com.

A file won't open — now what?

Open the built-in health check first: it explains what the file really is, whether it is readable, which encoding it uses and where the damaged regions are, so you know what to do next. If you are still stuck, email w4n9we1@gmail.com.
