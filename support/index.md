## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: Where can I download your apps?**
A: All apps are available on the [Apple App Store](/) — see the full list on the [homepage](/).

**Q: Are your apps free to use?**
A: Most apps are free to download with a one-time purchase option for premium features. Check the specific App Store listing for details.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: The app is crashing or freezing. What should I do?**
A: Please try the following steps:

1. Force close the app and reopen it.
2. Check the App Store for any available updates.
3. Restart your device.
4. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall the app (ensure your data is backed up if the app does not use iCloud sync).

**Q: How do I restore my purchases?**
A: Go to the app's **Settings** page and look for a "Restore Purchases" button. Tap it to restore any previously purchased premium features. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

### Privacy & Data

**Q: Do you collect my personal data?**
A: Privacy comes first. Most apps operate fully on-device. For details, review each app's Privacy Policy — linked from its [product page](/).
