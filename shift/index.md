# Shift

Shift is a private, local-first way to export and move your iPhone contacts.

## Export Contacts Your Way

Create VCF, PDF, CSV, XLSX, or HTML files in seconds. Choose the contacts you need, review the result, and share it through the standard iOS Share Sheet.

## Built Around Privacy

Your contacts and exported files are processed on your iPhone. Shift does not require an account, upload your contacts, or run advertising and cross-app tracking. Optional password-protected backups are encrypted locally on your device.

## Move With Confidence

Export now, keep a copy where you choose, and follow the restore guidance when you move to a new iPhone or another contacts service.
