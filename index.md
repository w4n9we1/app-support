# Lababacy Apps

Practical, privacy-first tools for iPhone — built and maintained by an independent developer.

Every app works on your device. Your files stay yours.

## On the App Store

### [Access Database Viewer: MDB AI](./accessviewer/)

Open and inspect Microsoft Access (MDB / ACCDB) databases right on your iPhone. A native C engine keeps large files fast, every file gets a health check before you open it, and nothing is ever uploaded.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760214171)

### [Shift: Contact Export & Backup](./shift/)

Export your contacts to VCF, CSV, XLSX, or an encrypted local backup — private, on-device, no account.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6790424072)

### [VoxMail - Speak to Text Email](./voxmail/)

Record a voice note, transcribe it on-device, and send it as a finished email — from speaking to a sent message.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6787936377)

### [Masked: Redact PDF & Photos](./masked/)

Remove sensitive faces, text, and metadata from photos, text, and PDFs before you share them — with AI or anyone else.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6791953842)

### [WholeNote: Export Notes](./note2poster/)

Turn Apple Notes into complete, searchable PDFs and long images — everything in one note, ready to archive or print.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6763737857)

### [MemoSnap: Lock Screen Todo](./memosnap/)

Put your to-dos and memo wallpapers on the lock screen — the reminder you literally cannot miss.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756552617)

### [NoNag: Block Open in App](./nonag/)

A Safari extension that automatically dismisses "Open in App" banners and redirect pop-ups — browse the web without being nagged into installing apps.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6776643459)

### [WallDone: Visual To-Do List](./walldone/)

Stick finished tasks on your wall and see progress pile up — a calm, visual alternative to deadline-driven lists.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6757802635)
